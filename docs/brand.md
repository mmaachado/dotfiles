# Brand

## Logo

The **droid head**, `assets/droid-head_flat.png`, is droid's logo: the README
header, this portal's logo and favicon.

## Codename art

Each major version has a codename after an Android dessert, and its art lives
at `assets/droid_<codename>.svg`. It's shown in the README, in
[Releases](releases.md) and in Settings → About.

| Series | Codename | Art |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.x | Cupcake | <img src="../assets/droid_cupcake.svg" alt="Cupcake" width="60"> |
| 2.x | Donut | *to come* |

## Colours

droid has no fixed palette: like Android 12+, the colours come from the
wallpaper or the theme (Material 3 dynamic colour, computed by
`kshelld scheme`). The portal and the apps' fallback use Android green,
`#3DDC84`.

## Type

- **Google Sans Flex** for the interface (OFL, Android 16's Material 3
  Expressive font).
- **Google Sans Code** for code and terminals (OFL).

## Icons

System icons are Material Symbols through
[droid-icons](https://github.com/mmaachado/droid-icons) (Rounded, Outlined or
Sharp, filled or not). App icons are Tela-circle.
