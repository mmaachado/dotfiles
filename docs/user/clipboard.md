# Clipboard

++meta+v++ opens the clipboard history, laid out like Windows 11's.

- Every copy is a card: text (up to three lines shown) or an **image**, with a thumbnail and its size.
- **Click** a card, or press ++enter++, to copy it back; then paste as usual.
- **Pin** what you use often (the pin button, a right-click or ++ctrl+p++). Pinned cards stay on top and survive *Clear all*.
- **Delete** a card with its button or ++delete++.
- Type to search the text copies.

Up to 200 copies are kept, 30 of them images. Copies that password managers
mark as secret are never stored.

## With your phone

With a phone paired in [KDE Connect](https://kdeconnect.kde.org), what you
copy on either side reaches the other: copies from the phone show up in the
history. Each card also has a **Send to phone** button. Turn the sync off in
Settings → Clipboard.
